# Product: GhostText

GhostText resurrects **Teletext** (Ceefax/Teletext-style TV text services, 1974–2012) as a modern web app with a Halloween twist.

- Every screen is a 40x24 character "page" with a 3-digit page number (100–899).
- Users navigate by typing page numbers or clicking the four colour "Fastext" buttons (red/green/yellow/cyan).
- Live content (news headlines, weather) is pulled from public sources and rewritten by an LLM into short, spooky Teletext-style pages.
- Target audience: retro-tech fans and people who want a calm, low-bandwidth, distraction-free news reader.
- Category: Kiroween 2026 — Resurrection.
